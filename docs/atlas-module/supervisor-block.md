This home is wired to an Atlas through `config/specs`, so these instructions bind here in addition to `AGENTS.md`.
The Atlas is the captain's map of each project's nodes, regions, and tickets, shown on the dashboard.
It is a map of work, never an authority over work: a ticket never grants an authority that `AGENTS.md` withholds.

Load `atlas-supervising` and `atlas-firstmate-bridge` at each of these points:
- at every intake, before you file, route, or dispatch the work;
- before you dispatch, land, or clean up ticketed work;
- at every heartbeat, after the fleet review that `AGENTS.md` requires;
- whenever an Atlas instruction and `AGENTS.md` seem to disagree.

`atlas-supervising` comes from the dashboard project and owns the ticket procedure.
`atlas-firstmate-bridge` owns every supervisor rule for how that procedure composes with this home, and it wins where the two differ.
